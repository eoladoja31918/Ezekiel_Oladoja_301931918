# Case Study: Hospital / Clinic Management System

## Organisation / Scenario
A simulated hospital/clinic that manages patient care and clinical records.

## Nature of the Business
The hospital provides medical consultations, diagnosis, treatment,
prescriptions, and laboratory services.

## Database Users
Receptionists, doctors, nurses, laboratory staff, and administrators.

## Major Data Stored
Patient details, doctor details, appointments, diagnoses,
prescriptions, laboratory requests, and laboratory results.

## Key Operations
- Register patients.
- Book appointments with doctors.
- Record patient diagnoses.
- Issue prescriptions.
- Request laboratory tests and record results.
- Retrieve patient medical history.
